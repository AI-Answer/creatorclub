# Brand assets

Creator Compiler's original mark is in `docs/brand/` and the `BrandMark` component. Service marks are bundled in `recorder/app/public/brands/` and rendered by `BrandLogo`; they load locally, including in packaged desktop apps. Light and dark monochrome variants keep the OpenAI and X marks legible without recolouring the other service marks.

| Service | Official source | Included asset |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claude | [Claude homepage](https://claude.com/) | The clay asterisk path from the site's SVG lockup, with its original geometry and colour. |
| ChatGPT / Codex sign-in | [OpenAI design guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/) | The black and white Blossom paths from the official brand SVGs; the surrounding spacing-diagram guides are omitted. The connection is labelled ChatGPT because it uses the person's ChatGPT sign-in. |
| Gemini | [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) | Google's [512 px colour icon](https://www.gstatic.com/lamda/images/gemini_sparkle_4g_512_lt_f94943af3be039176192d.png). |
| Postiz | [Postiz](https://postiz.com/) | The symbol paths from its [current website lockup](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/logo.7379cc96.svg), displayed on a dark tile so the white paper and transparent letter remain visible. |
| YouTube | [YouTube icon resources](https://brand.youtube/youtube-icon) | The original red digital PNG from the [official icon download](https://www.gstatic.com/marketing-cms/78/29/3e68a1414bb28d0b7e47b44c3c91/youtube-icon.zip). |
| X | [X brand toolkit](https://about.x.com/en/who-we-are/brand-toolkit) | The site's X SVG symbol in black and white. |
| Reddit | [Reddit brand resources](https://redditinc.com/brand) | The site's [full-colour Snoo favicon](https://redditinc.com/hubfs/Reddit_Favicon_FullColor_48x48.png). |

Retrieved 2026-10-31. Service marks identify their respective integrations and remain the property of their owners. Creator Compiler's product identity remains separate.

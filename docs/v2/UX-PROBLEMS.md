# CreatorClub UX problems (live app, 1440x900, Samin's data, 2026-10-29)

Visible controls per screen (buttons, links, inputs, tabs): Overview 21, Ideas 32, Working now 35, Signals 35, Production 27, Video Board 43, Plan 47, Record 70, Edit 70, Publish 41, Settings 28.

1. **Three navigation systems on the video page.** The top tabs (Board, Plan, Record, Edit, Publish), a second step bar at the bottom with the same five steps, and a scene strip under the header, all beside the app sidebar.
2. **The script appears three times on Record:** on the scene card, in the right panel, and in Plan.
3. **Record buries its one job.** The Record button is greyed out unless you pick the scene first, among four source rows (camera, microphone, screen, computer audio) with toggles, dropdowns, gears and preview buttons, and presets.
4. **Edit is a second app inside the app.** It has its own header (Ask Claude, Import, undo, redo, history, export), a 9-item tool rail, two toolbars, transport, a layer toolbar and a resizer. 51 fixed controls, plus 20–40 fields once a clip is clicked. It opens empty when the take is not on the timeline. The Settings gear is unreachable inside it.
5. **The timeline is rough.** Roughly one row visible by default, effect rows you can only click, no linked screen, camera and audio, a zoom that jumps, no ticks, no markers, clips that can overlap, and any background save cancels a drag.
6. **No motion graphics.** Only six static elements. "Style video" proposes a layout and three plain text slates.
7. **Two unrelated chats.** Board chat (suggests only) and Edit chat (edits only), each knowing nothing about the other steps, and neither docked.
8. **Thumbnails without information.** Ideas shows several cards with the same source thumbnail, three actions each, and three rating chips on every card.
9. **Settings and the Publish step are unclear.** Publish only prepares text and an export; nothing publishes.
10. **Wording leaks.** "[YOUR CALL: …]" markers in older plans, and jargon such as GGML in errors.

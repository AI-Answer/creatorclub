# CreatorClub UX principles

For the creator who goes from an idea to a published video on one computer, mostly alone. Every screen is judged against these. If a change breaks one, it needs a reason written next to it.

## The path
Ideas and market signals (Board) → plan the video (Plan) → record it (Record) → edit it by asking Claude (Edit) → publish (Publish). Settings holds anything you set up once.

## Principles

1. **One next step per screen.** Each step has one black button that moves you forward ("Continue to recording"). Everything else is quieter: an outline button, then plain text. If two things look equally important, one of them is wrong.
2. **Say what happens, in plain words.** Name the result, not the machinery: "Cut the pauses", not "Auto-cut". No "package", "outline", "take", "canvas" unless the creator used the word first.
3. **The creator's words are editable where they are.** Ideas, notes, titles, hooks, scenes and scripts are rich text. Click, type, it saves on its own and says so. No separate edit mode, no raw text boxes.
4. **Setup never blocks the work.** AI sign-in, the Postiz key and other keys live in Settings. A step that needs one says so in one line and offers a button that opens Settings. Nothing asks for a key in the middle of a task.
5. **Few controls, the rest one click away.** At most five visible tools in a group. The rest sit under "More". Rare things never share a row with common ones.
6. **Ask, don't hunt.** Every step has Ask Claude with three suggestions for that step. Typing "/" lists a short set of the creator's own skills; typing a name finds any other.
7. **The AI proposes, the creator decides.** A change from Claude arrives as a card that can be previewed, applied and undone. Nothing is applied silently.
8. **Always show the state.** Saved, saving, recording, exporting, waiting. A refusal says why and what to do ("Clips stay joined end to end. Drag one onto another to change the order").
9. **Everything undoes.** Undo and redo are always reachable. Deleting says what was deleted and offers undo.
10. **Local first.** The work lives in files on this computer. The creator signs in with their own Claude or Codex. Nothing needs a cloud account to work, except publishing, which the creator chooses.
11. **One look.** Colours, spacing and type come from the tokens. One primary, one outline, one quiet style. Icons from one set. No decoration that carries no meaning.
12. **Check it like a user.** A change is not done until it has been used in the real app and looked at.

## How we check a screen
- Can a first-time creator say what to do next within five seconds?
- Count the visible controls in each group. More than five: move some under More.
- Is any word the creator would not say?
- Is anything asked for that could wait for Settings?
- Does every action show a result and offer undo?

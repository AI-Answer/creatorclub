# Borumi (research agent, 2026-10-29). Installed at /Applications/Borumi.app 0.32.1. Notes in CC/docs/reference/borumi.md.

Copy:
1. Takes keep themselves: Confirm puts it on the timeline, Archive keeps it out of the way.
2. Scene cards are the recording spine: each has its own Record button and take thumbnails.
3. One shared bottom bar: Back to X, steps, one "Continue to Y".
4. Take preview has exactly three choices: Delete take, Record another take, Continue.
5. One pre-flight card for sources; a clear block until fixed.
6. Transcript beside the timeline: deleting words cuts the video, the spoken word highlights, words sit on the waveform.
7. Automations menu with settings (silence removal aggressiveness and padding).
8. Version history for every AI edit, with Restore this version.
9. Plain preview-quality notice: "Previewing in lower quality without effects".
10. Favorites in caption and layout style galleries.

Editor facts: left tool rail (Canvas, Layout, Clip, Zoom, Elements, Music, Captions, Cursor, AI). Timeline has select, blade, snap, ripple, align, zoom. Hover-scrub previews frames without moving the playhead. Per-clip speed up to 20x. Alignment guides. Layout is a clip on its own lane. Auto zoom follows the cursor, with focus modes (cursor, point, face). Silence removal is in an Automations menu. Captions have a style gallery. Elements have entrance and exit transitions. Export can be full, one scene, or a range. Borumi MCP works with Claude Desktop, ChatGPT Desktop and Claude Code, with version history for AI edits. No publish or YouTube step.

Avoid: low-level device settings, locking export behind a paywall, file-only projects, AI edits with vague scope, an AI companion that needs a separate app.
Unconfirmed: recording bar visuals, whether styles preview before applying.

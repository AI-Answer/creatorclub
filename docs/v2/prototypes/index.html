CreatorClub v2: three layouts to choose from

Same pipeline in all three (discover, plan, record, edit, publish), same companion chat docked on the right, same Samin data, same look. They differ in how much the screen shows and who drives. Open one, click through every step, and try the timeline in Edit (drag, trim, split with S, delete, M for a marker, scroll with ⌘ to zoom).

A

Guided path

One task per screen, one big action, one thin step bar. Best for beginners.

  • Record: one red button and a one-line source status
  • Edit: big stage, transcript, rich timeline, "Auto-edit and style" side sheet with live previews
  • Cut: Overview, Signals, Production and a separate Ideas screen
Open prototype A
B

Studio

Scene rail, stage and companion, with a dock that changes by step. Best for power users.

  • Scenes rail is the script outline, the take status and the transcript
  • Inspector appears only when you select a clip or block
  • Cost: denser; relies on hover and selection to hide controls
Open prototype B
C

Conversation-first

The companion drives; the canvas shows what it makes. Fewest buttons.

  • One conversation across all steps, with result cards you can apply, preview and undo
  • Version strip ("v1 · Recorded scenes 1 to 3") and History always visible
  • Risk: conversation can hide state

Everything shared across the three

  • Companion docked right on every step, shows what it is looking at, acts with Undo, one conversation per video, ⌘K palette.
  • Record: takes keep themselves. Continue puts the take on the timeline and moves to the next scene.
  • Edit: transcript editing, linked screen/camera/audio, snapping, ripple delete, markers, versions, quick preview of styles and auto-edits, motion graphics blocks.
  • Publish: Postiz first (your API key), "Upload straight to YouTube" second.
  • Each prototype has an About drawer tagging every feature Exists, Needs work or New.

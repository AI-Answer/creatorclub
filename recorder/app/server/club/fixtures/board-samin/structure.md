# How Samin's videos are built

Counted by reading his 9 own transcripts (video ids in brackets). Section shares are estimates. These are habits to start from, not rules: he decides.

## Shape 1: bold claim, then three levels (6 of 9)
[0VghkmXfE2o, 4TbWTgyzNl0, BTVpef58xMY, cTFgEqOcUEo, lH5wrfNwL3k, loosely 1LWPkLG6bP0]
1. Claim and roadmap (about 5%): the outcome, what it cost him or what it replaces, then "three levels".
2. Why it matters (5 to 17%): the problem, with numbers or a money case, and an honest caveat.
3. A short reassurance that no technical background is needed, then "let's get into it".
4. Level 1 (about 12 to 25%): what the thing is (with an everyday analogy first), setup, the first test, and what it cost.
5. Level 2 (about 20 to 25%): a real workflow with a spoken prompt shown on screen.
6. Level 3 (15 to 50%): scale it up, package it, monetise it, or automate it.
7. Ending: a pointer to another video. No recap.
The first hands-on step comes at about 15 to 30% of the video.

## Shape 2: opinion with demos (2 of 9)
[XqnYcLC45iE, caj4n9Vx54o]
A news item or a live demo opens it, then a long argument (cost, or useful versus flashy), then a demo that backs it up, then a short pitch. Only one of the 9 videos opens on a news event.

## Shape 3: tour, then concepts, then setup (1 of 9)
[_ZehNseg0Qg]
No levels. Opening with the open questions, the backstory, a tour of the app, the concepts (analogies), the setup, a build, a longer demo.

## Habits that repeat
- A spoken prompt is shown on screen when it is used.
- Each demo goes: start it, "I'll come back", it got done, what it cost.
- A cost figure appears in most of the tutorials.
- Every concept gets an everyday analogy before the detail.
- The end is a pointer to another video, not a summary.

## What his videos do not do
- No hook, setup and points template, no numbered point list.
- No "Remember" line per point.
- No single yes or no question carrying the whole video.
- Length does not match the topic: the 9 videos run from 2,607 to 18,739 words.

## Topics that produce these shapes
- A newly launched model or tool, and what you can build with it.
- Making money with AI video or content workflows.
- Cost and subscription versus API.
- Agent teams and personal setups.
- Finance automation.
Titles are bold claims or fixes: "Just Changed X Forever", "CANCEL X, Do This Instead", "Better Than 99% of People", "X is TOO EXPENSIVE, Here's the Fix".

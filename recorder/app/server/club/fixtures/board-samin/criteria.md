# How ideas are chosen

The "Find new ideas" check reads this file every time. Edit it to change what gets proposed.

## Who the videos are for
Samin Yasar's viewers: non-technical people learning to use AI tools and getting good at building with several new ones. They are not developers. Their three problems: no AI employee working for them yet, falling behind on new tools, learning alone without good resources. Their three wrong beliefs: "I need to be super technical", "it takes too much time", "I can't learn this".

## An idea needs all of these
1. **Timely or evergreen.**
   - Timely: something happened in the last 7 days (launch, update, incident, price change) and you confirmed it on an official page or a reliable news source you opened.
   - Evergreen: a format that is already working for other creators, or a question viewers keep asking. It must still make sense in 6 months.
2. **Demand.** Evidence in the context: videos on the topic beating their channel's usual views, fast views per day, or viewers asking. No evidence means rate it "mid" and say "not checked".
3. **Gap.** How many recent videos already cover it, and whether they are news explainers or practical how-tos. A practical how-to with nothing big out there is an open gap.
4. **Can he show it.** Samin can demonstrate it with his own hands on screen. Do not propose anything that needs a result he does not have.
5. **Fits his viewer.** It breaks one of the three beliefs or solves one of the three problems, in plain words.
6. **Title.** A working title of 7 words or fewer, naming one specific thing, accurate. His titles are bold claims or fixes ("Just Changed X Forever", "CANCEL X, Do This Instead", "X is TOO EXPENSIVE, Here's the Fix"), not yes or no questions. No income or return claims. No "will make you rich".
7. **Can be built in levels.** There is more than one thing to show, from an easy first test to a bigger workflow. A single yes or no test is not enough for one of his videos: turn it into a build or a setup, or leave it out.
8. **Honest facts.** Every number in a note comes from the context or a page you opened. Anything unverified says so.

## Rating (shown as the three icons on a card)
- demand: high, mid or low
- gap: high means open (little competition), low means crowded
- show: high means easy to show, low means hard
- Mark `top` only when all three are high and it fits his viewer.

## What to avoid
- Ideas already on the board (any status).
- Clickbait that misstates a fact ("AI hacked the internet").
- Security or legal advice he cannot back up.
- Mentioning any business or product of Dani's. These videos are only about Samin's channel.
- Videos that depend on a news peg older than 10 days, unless an evergreen core carries them.

## Lanes
- `evergreen`, `timely`, or `both` (an evergreen core with a timely peg).

## Learn from results
If published videos are listed in the context with their 7-day views against his usual, prefer the kinds of idea that beat his usual and avoid the kinds that fell short.

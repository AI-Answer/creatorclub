Rules
- Everything must come from the transcripts. Quotes must be verbatim (captions may contain errors; keep them) and short. Do not give counts or percentages: you cannot count reliably, so say "often", "sometimes" or "once".
- Say which video each quote comes from using its id in square brackets.
- Plain words. No praise.
- Leave out any call to join a club, community, course or product. When you describe how videos end, describe a pointer to another video, or the kind of sign-off, and nothing else.

Write markdown with these sections:

# {name}: Voice Guide
## 1. Summary
Who the creator talks to, how they sound, what they promise, in 4 to 6 sentences.
## 2. Structure
How videos open, the roadmap or promise, how long until the first hands-on or main step, how a step runs, transitions, how videos end.
## 3. Sentence and word habits
Rhythm, openers, check-ins and tag questions, instruction frames, hedges, intensifiers, fillers, how terms are explained, how the viewer is addressed. Quote real examples.
## 4. Analogies
The kinds of everyday comparison the creator uses and how they set them up.
## 5. Demos and examples
How they introduce and run a demo.
## 6. What they avoid
Things the transcripts never do (only if clearly absent).
## 7. Ids
List of video ids used.
